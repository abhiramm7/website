\section*{Positions}

\years{2025--}%
\noindent\textbf{Lead Data Scientist} at Inframark\\[0.1cm]
Lead Inframark's data science team and work with product, executive leadership, and utility operations to build the machine learning models behind its digital water products.

\vspace{2mm}


{\small \textbf{Key Highlights:}}
\vspace{-2mm}
\begin{itemize}
	\setlength\itemsep{1mm}
	\item \textbf{Platform Machine Learning}: Lead the design and development of Inframark's shared ML platform, covering data ingestion, model training, deployment, and retraining.
	\item \textbf{Chemical Dosing Optimization}: Deployed ML-based dosing control for phosphorus removal and chlorine disinfection across multiple utilities. The chlorine residual model in production predicts the residual to within $\pm$0.10 mg/L; the phosphorus system pairs a 1D-CNN effluent forecaster with a dose optimizer and detects sensor drift from model residuals before it causes compliance misses.
	\item \textbf{Inflow \& Infiltration Detection}: Lead the development of a system that detects and quantifies I\&I in collection systems from pump station and in-network sensor data, attributes wet-weather flow to specific pipe segments, and ranks rehabilitation candidates by projected I\&I volume reduction. For a client in Georgia, this approach has reduced the network area flagged for infiltration investigation by 60\%.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}

\years{2023--2025}%
\noindent\textbf{Senior Data Scientist} at Xylem\\[0.1cm]
Developed the machine learning platform for Xylem's digital water products.

\vspace{2mm}


{\small \textbf{Key Highlights:}}
\vspace{-2mm}
\begin{itemize}
	\setlength\itemsep{1mm}
	\item Led the development of statistical and machine learning methods for time-series filtering and anomaly detection, used for predictive maintenance and plant operations.
	\item Built an MLOps platform on Flyte that Xylem's product teams used to train, deploy, and maintain their models. The same platform ran large SWMM simulations at scale on AWS clusters for optimization studies and model development.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2mm}

\years{2020--2023}%
\noindent\textbf{Hydraulic Control and Optimization Engineer} at Xylem\\[0.1cm]
Built machine learning and data engineering systems for Xylem's wastewater network optimization products, used daily by utility operators.

\vspace{2mm}


{\small \textbf{Key Highlights:}}
\vspace{-2mm}
\begin{itemize}
	\setlength\itemsep{1mm}
	\item \textbf{Simulation Platform}: Maintained a SWMM simulation platform used for master planning and for developing control strategies.
	\item \textbf{Model Calibration}: Developed a calibration method that generates inflows to a SWMM network from observed hydrological data, a more accurate way of representing flow generation than SWMM's built-in hydrology module. Wrote a custom pollutant-based tracer analysis that estimates travel times from each subcatchment to the calibration points.
	\item \textbf{Control Logic Development}: Designed control logic that coordinates storage and release across distributed stormwater assets, including BMPs, to improve treatment and shave peak flows.
	\item \textbf{Real-Time Control}: Helped develop a real-time controller for an in-line storage site in a combined sewer system. During wet weather it closes the site's gates to hold flow in the existing pipes. Wrote the control logic and reviewed the complete implementation on the site controller.
	\item \textbf{Time Series Forecasting}: Developed a 1D-CNN model that combines NOAA rainfall forecasts with near-real-time flow measurements to predict 24-hour inflow to wastewater treatment plants.
	\item \textbf{Geospatial Time Series Analytics}: Built a 1D-CNN interpolation framework for spatially distributed river level data, used for environmental monitoring and regulatory compliance reporting.
	\item \textbf{Anomaly Detection}: Designed a real-time processing system, built on symbolic programming and statistical methods, that detects network irregularities across 600+ concurrent data streams.
	\item \textbf{Operational Dashboards}: Built dashboards that put model output in front of operators and the public, including predictive treatment plant inflow for operators and the City of Richmond's \href{https://www.rva.gov/public-utilities/csooutfalls}{public CSO notification map}. Built the analytics and backend behind the Richmond map.
	\item \textbf{Backend Management}: Maintained the real-time services, ETL scripts, and internal databases behind Xylem Vue's Wastewater Network Optimization Solution, and worked with the integrations team to build ingestion pipelines for MQTT sensor feeds.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Open Source Software}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{itemize}
	\setlength\itemsep{1mm}
	\item \href{https://klabum.github.io/pystorms/}{\texttt{pystorms}} (creator): simulation sandbox with curated scenarios for designing and benchmarking stormwater control algorithms.
	\item \href{https://github.com/OpenWaterAnalytics/Stormwater-Management-Model}{\texttt{SWMM}} and \href{https://github.com/OpenWaterAnalytics/pyswmm}{\texttt{pyswmm}} (maintainer): EPA's Stormwater Management Model, the standard model for stormwater systems, and its Python interface.
	\item \href{https://github.com/kLabUM/StormReactor}{\texttt{StormReactor}} (co-author): integrated modeling of water quality and water balance in EPA-SWMM simulations.
	\item \href{https://github.com/kLabUM/rrcf}{\texttt{rrcf}} (contributor): Robust Random Cut Forest algorithm for anomaly detection on streaming data.
	\item \href{https://github.com/abhiramm7/openfloodhub}{\texttt{OpenFloodHub}} (creator): an open alternative to Google Flood Hub. Trains a small CNN per stream gauge on that gauge's history and forecasts the next 12 hours of streamflow, mapped against flood thresholds for Washington, DC.
	\item \href{https://github.com/abhiramm7/dc-flood-sim}{\texttt{dc-flood-sim}} (creator): a city flood model that runs shallow-water physics in the browser on the GPU, built from USGS lidar terrain, OpenStreetMap buildings, and live USGS gauge inflows.
\end{itemize}
